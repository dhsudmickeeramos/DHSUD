Administrative and Finance Division (AFD)

The Administrative and Finance Division (AFD) is a core support division of the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) that provides essential administrative, financial, human resource, records, procurement, property, and general support services. It ensures the efficient management of the Department’s administrative and financial resources, supports the implementation of programs and operations, and facilitates compliance with government policies, rules, and regulations.
